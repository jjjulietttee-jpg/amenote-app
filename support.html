Common questions

  • Does it work without internet? Yes. After the speech model downloads once, recording, transcripts and notes work offline.
  • My app closed during a sermon. Amenote saves audio every few seconds. Open the app again and tap “Make notes” on the recovered recording.
  • Restore purchases. Settings → Restore purchases, or on the Pro screen.
  • Cancel a subscription. Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Amenote.
  • Which Bibles are included? King James Version and Berean Standard Bible (both public domain).
