I bought Pro and it says Free

Settings inside Amenote, then Restore purchases. Use the Apple ID that paid. If it still shows Free after a minute online, email the purchase date.

It will not transcribe

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition for Amenote in iPhone Settings. Pick your language under Recognition language; the first run may need a one-time language download over Wi-Fi.

Recording stopped when the screen locked or a call came in

An incoming call or another app taking the microphone pauses capture. Short chunks are saved as you go, so what was heard before the interruption is kept. Start a new recording for the rest.

Can I use a recording I already have?

Yes: use the import button on the home screen and choose an audio file (m4a, mp3, wav and similar). Processing happens on the phone and may take a while for a long file.

A verse is wrong or missing

References are found by reading the transcript, so a mumbled "Romans eight" can be lost. Look the passage up yourself in the app's Bible reader, and tell us about repeating cases.

Which Bible is inside?

King James Version and Berean Standard Bible. Switch under Translation in Settings. Both work with no connection.

How do I delete a sermon or cancel Pro?

Sermon: open it, menu, Delete sermon. Subscription: iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Amenote. The lifetime plan has nothing to cancel.

I want the weekly reminder at another time

Settings, then Weekly reminder; choose the day and the hour.
