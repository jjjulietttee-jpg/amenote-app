Where your sermons go (nowhere)

Privacy statement, October 6, 2026

The short answer

Amenote has no sign-up and no servers of its own. The recording, the written transcript, the key points and your own notes are stored inside the app on your iPhone. We cannot hear, read or recover them.

Microphone and speech

The app asks for the microphone to capture the sermon, saved as short audio chunks in its private storage. It also asks for Speech Recognition. Transcription is set to run on the device, so it keeps working in airplane mode. The first time you use a language, iOS may download that language's speech model once from Apple; after that, no audio is involved in any network request.

The note-writing step

Key points and weekly practices are produced by Apple's built-in on-device language model through the system's Foundation Models framework, and not by a cloud AI service. If your iPhone cannot run it, the app falls back to a simpler local summary. Either way the text stays on the phone.

What we collect: nothing of yours

The app contains no analytics kit, no advertising code and no crash reporter from a third party. We do not see which sermons you record, which verses you look up, or what you search for.

Buying Pro

Purchases are made through Apple's StoreKit. The payment is handled on Apple's side, and Apple's answer tells the app if Pro is on. Amenote itself never receives your card, name or Apple ID. Apple's own privacy policy covers what it records about the purchase.

Reminders

The optional weekly "Live it this week" nudge and the trial-end reminder are notifications scheduled on your phone. They are not sent from a server.

Getting rid of things

One sermon
Open it, use the menu, and choose Delete sermon. That removes its audio files and its notes together.
Everything
Delete Amenote from the home screen; the library goes with it.
Permissions
Turn Microphone, Speech Recognition and Notifications off in iPhone Settings, Amenote, whenever you like.

Backups and shared files

If your iPhone is backed up, Apple may carry the app's data along under your own account. A file you export or share leaves under your control, not ours.

Recording other people

A sermon often has a congregation in it. Ask your church before recording, and keep audio private unless the speaker agrees otherwise.

Young users and updates

The app is for general audiences and gathers no personal details from anyone. If this statement changes, the new text appears here with a fresh date. Questions: nastya.shchaveleva@internet.ru.
