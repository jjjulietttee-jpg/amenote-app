Privacy Policy

Amenote: Sermon Notes & Bible · Last updated September 23, 2026

Amenote records sermons and turns them into notes. It is built so that your recordings and notes stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no server that stores your sermons, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • Sermon recordings (audio), transcripts, key points, verses, weekly practices, questions and your own notes.
  • Audio files you import from Files. Amenote keeps a copy in its own storage on your iPhone and never uploads it.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while you record a sermon. Speech is turned into text on your device with Apple's on-device speech recognition. The first time, iOS may download the language model from Apple; after that, recording and transcription work offline. Audio is not sent to us or to any third party.

Notes written on your device

Key points, practices and questions are written on your iPhone with Apple's on-device language model (Apple Intelligence) when it is available, or by Amenote's own text analysis when it is not. Nothing is sent to an external AI service. Bible verses come from the texts built into the app (King James Version and Berean Standard Bible, public domain); the model never writes Scripture.

Notifications

If you turn on the weekly reminder or start a free trial, Amenote schedules local notifications on your iPhone. They are not sent through a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Amenote receives only the status of your purchase, never your payment details. We may use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier and purchase status to validate purchases; it never receives your recordings or notes.

Analytics and tracking

Amenote does not track you across apps or websites and does not use advertising identifiers.

Deleting your data

Delete a sermon from its menu in the app, or delete Amenote from your iPhone to remove all of its data.

Children

Amenote is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
